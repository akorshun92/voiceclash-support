Frequently asked questions

Why does Voice Clash need microphone and speech recognition access?

Voice Clash listens to you say the word shown on screen and uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework to check whether you said it correctly. Without microphone and speech recognition permission, the core gameplay can't work.

Why does the app ask for local network access?

Battle mode lets you play against another phone on the same Wi-Fi network. Local network access is used only to discover and connect to nearby devices for that mode — no data leaves your local network.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." If you bought Premium or a word pack on this Apple ID before, it will be unlocked again automatically at no extra cost.

Can I create my own word lists?

Yes — Voice Clash lets you build custom categories with your own words, in addition to the 24+ built-in categories across 8 languages.

I found a bug or a translation mistake in a word pack. How do I report it?

Please email oleksandr.korshun.apps@gmail.com with the category name, the word, and the language — we review and fix word pack issues regularly.
