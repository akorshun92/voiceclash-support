Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Voice Clash ("the app") is developed by Oleksandr Korshun. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Summary

Voice Clash has no user accounts, and we (the developer) do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The free version shows non-personalized ads served by Google AdMob, which receives limited device and usage data to deliver those ads (see Advertising). Premium removes ads. We do not use analytics SDKs.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

Voice Clash uses your device's microphone so you can pronounce words during gameplay. Audio is processed using Apple's speech recognition framework solely to check whether the word you said matches the word shown on screen. Recognition runs on your device using Apple's speech models; depending on your device and iOS settings, Apple may process it as described in Apple's own privacy policy. Voice Clash itself does not record, store, or transmit your voice audio beyond that in-the-moment check, and audio is never shared with advertisers.

Local Network Access

Battle Arena mode allows you to play against up to 8 devices on the same Wi-Fi network. The app uses local network discovery (Bonjour) only to find and connect to nearby devices running Voice Clash for this feature. No gameplay data is sent outside your local network.

Advertising

The free version of Voice Clash shows ads from Google AdMob outside gameplay. Premium removes them.

  • The app requests non-personalized ads only. Ads are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • Voice Clash does not ask for permission to track you, does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with data brokers.
  • To deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google may receive information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating system type, app version, and interactions with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We do not send Google your voice, your custom words or pictures, or your purchase details.

Where local law requires it, the app asks for your choice before it requests any ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google appears when you first open the app. In US states with applicable privacy laws, that form instead lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. If either applies to you, a Privacy options row appears in the app's Settings, where you can review or change your answer at any time.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (Premium unlock and word pack bundles) are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data Stored On Your Device

Custom word and picture categories you create (including photos you choose to add from your library), your match history and personal bests, your settings (timer length, language, etc.), and purchase/unlock status are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Photos are only read when you pick them yourself; the app does not access the rest of your photo library.

Children's Privacy

Voice Clash does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads shown in the app are non-personalized, so they are not targeted based on a child's behavior. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
