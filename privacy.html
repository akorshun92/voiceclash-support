Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Voice Clash ("the app") is developed by Oleksandr Korshun. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Summary

Voice Clash has no user accounts, and we (the developer) do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The free version offers optional, non-personalized rewarded ads served by Google AdMob, which receives limited device and usage data to deliver those ads (see Advertising). Premium turns them off. We do not use analytics SDKs.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

Voice Clash uses your device's microphone so you can pronounce words during gameplay. Audio is processed using Apple's speech recognition framework solely to check whether the word you said matches the word shown on screen. Recognition runs on your device using Apple's speech models; depending on your device and iOS settings, Apple may process it as described in Apple's own privacy policy.

The mini-games use the microphone in a few different ways, all on your device. Copycat checks your sound imitation with Apple's built-in sound classifier (SoundAnalysis). Shout measures how loud you are, and Pitch Match measures the pitch of your voice. The other mini-games check your spoken answers with the same speech recognition described above. None of them sends audio anywhere.

Voice Clash itself does not record, store, or transmit your voice audio beyond that in-the-moment check, and audio is never shared with advertisers.

Local Network Access

Arena mode allows you to play against up to 8 devices on the same Wi-Fi network. The app uses local network discovery (Bonjour) only to find and connect to nearby devices running Voice Clash for this feature. No gameplay data is sent outside your local network.

Advertising

The only ads in Voice Clash are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: you choose to watch one to unlock something for a limited time, such as a couple of matches with a Premium word pack, a past Daily Challenge, a few plays of a mini-game, or a Copycat sound group. Rewarded ads only play when you tap to watch them, never during gameplay. Premium turns them off.

  • The app requests non-personalized ads only. Ads are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • After any consent form (below), iOS asks whether Voice Clash may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not available to the app or to Google, and the game works the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Voice Clash does not share data with data brokers.
  • To deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google may receive information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating system type, app version, and interactions with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We do not send Google your voice, your custom words or pictures, or your purchase details.

Where local law requires it, the app asks for your choice before it requests any ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google appears when you first open the app. In US states with applicable privacy laws, that form instead lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. If either applies to you, a Privacy options row appears in the app's Settings, where you can review or change your answer at any time.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (the Premium unlock and individual word packs) are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data Stored On Your Device

Custom word and picture categories you create (including photos you choose to add from your library), your match history and personal bests, your settings (timer length, language, etc.), and purchase/unlock status are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Photos are only read when you pick them yourself; the app does not access the rest of your photo library.

Children's Privacy

Voice Clash does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads shown in the app are non-personalized, so they are not targeted based on a child's behavior. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
