Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Voice Clash ("the app") is developed by Oleksandr Korshun ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Summary

Voice Clash does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and there are no user accounts.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

Voice Clash uses your device's microphone so you can pronounce words during gameplay. Audio is processed using Apple's on-device or system Speech Recognition framework (SFSpeechRecognizer) solely to check whether the word you said matches the word shown on screen. Depending on your device and iOS settings, this processing may happen entirely on-device or via Apple's speech recognition service — Voice Clash itself does not record, store, transmit, or have access to your voice audio beyond that in-the-moment check.

Local Network Access

Battle mode allows you to play against another device on the same Wi-Fi network. The app uses local network discovery (Bonjour) only to find and connect to nearby devices running Voice Clash for this feature. No gameplay data is sent outside your local network.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (Premium unlock and word pack bundles) are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Data Stored On Your Device

Custom word categories you create, your settings (timer length, language, etc.), and purchase/unlock status are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Voice Clash does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected at all, the app does not present privacy risks specific to children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
